# Kapaat 2.0.0

**Released 4 November 2026.** A rewrite of Kapaat around a smaller, clearer idea: know what's in every bin, find it from your phone, and print labels that survive the garage.

v2 replaces the single-file v1 app with three containers (Postgres, an API, and an installable web app). Your v1 data comes across with a one-time importer, and **your old printed QR labels keep working**.

## What's new

### Organise the way your home is laid out
- **Rooms › zones › bins.** A room is an area (Garage), a zone is part of it (North wall shelves), bins live in zones. Manage them under **Places**.
- **Add items first, sort later.** Items don't need a bin. Find them under *Not in a bin* and put them away when you're ready.
- **Bulk move.** Select any number of items and move them to another bin, or take them out of their bin, in one step.

### Onboard and offboard
- **New bins get their label straight away.** Creating a bin offers its printable label immediately.
- **Retire instead of delete.** Items are retired with a reason (used up, donated, sold, thrown away, lost); bins are retired with their contents either moved to another bin or taken out. Everything stays in history and can be restored.

### Check out and check in
- Mark items as checked out, with an optional due date and note ("lent to Sam"). A *Checked out* filter shows what's away; check items back in from the item page or by scanning.
- Every item has a history: added, moved, checked out and in, edited, retired, restored, and who did it.

### Labels that last
- Exact-size PDFs with a crisp vector QR code, the bin code, its name and location.
- **Default: 48 × 30 mm**, sized to sit under 48 mm clear packing tape.
- Also: Brother DK 62 mm rolls, Avery L7160 (A4, 21 per sheet) and 5160 (Letter, 30 per sheet), and plain A4 / Letter paper with crop marks and dashed cut lines (24 / 21 per sheet).
- Reuse partly used sticker sheets by skipping used positions; print several copies of each label. Kapaat remembers the size you picked.

### A real phone app
- **Install from the browser** (Add to Home Screen) with Kapaat's own icon.
- **Scan** a bin or item label with the camera to open it, **batch move** items by scanning them and then the destination bin, or **batch check in** returned items.
- Your last-seen inventory stays readable **offline**. Changes need a connection.
- Light and dark themes follow your device.

### Simple, safe sign-in
- Sign in with a **6-digit code sent by email**. No passwords to manage.
- The first person to sign in becomes admin (pin it with `ADMIN_EMAIL`); admins **invite** everyone else, or turn on open sign-up.
- Optional **passkeys** (Face ID, Touch ID, device PIN) and **single sign-on** (Authentik, Keycloak, Google, any OIDC provider).
- **Per-person API tokens**, read-only or read/write, for scripts and home automation.

### Google Sheets, without n8n
- Kapaat can send every change to a webhook. A [copy-paste Google Apps Script](../sheets-webhook.md) keeps a spreadsheet mirror with *Items*, *Bins* and *Log* tabs.
- Deliveries are signed, retried with backoff, and always arrive in order. **Send full snapshot** refreshes the whole sheet. Works with n8n, Home Assistant or your own code too.

### Also
- Photos for items and bins (re-encoded on upload, which strips location data).
- Tags with colours, categories, search across names, tags, categories and bins.
- CSV export, protected against spreadsheet formula injection.
- Interactive API docs at `/api/docs`.

## Breaking changes

- **New deployment.** v2 runs as `db` + `api` + `web` containers from `ghcr.io/nomi25home/kapaat-api` and `ghcr.io/nomi25home/kapaat-web`. Use the new [`docker-compose.yml`](../../docker-compose.yml) and [`.env.example`](../../.env.example).
- **New required settings:** `SECRET_KEY` and `POSTGRES_PASSWORD`. `AUTH_SECRET_KEY`, `SERVICE_API_KEY`, `ALLOWED_EMAILS`, `DB_PATH`, the n8n settings and SQLCipher settings are gone.
- **Removed:** SQLite and SQLCipher, Litestream, the n8n workflow and sync conflicts, two-way Sheets sync, XLSX export.
- **People aren't migrated.** Invite them again from *More → People*; they sign in with the same email address.

## Upgrading from v1

1. Back up v1 (its *Download full backup* button, or copy the Docker volume).
2. Start v2 from the README and sign in once.
3. Run the importer **dry run** against a copy of your v1 data, read its warnings, then run it for real. Re-running is safe.

Full steps: [docs/migration-from-v1.md](../migration-from-v1.md).

v1 stays available as `ghcr.io/nomi25home/kapaat:1` if you need to stay on it for now.
